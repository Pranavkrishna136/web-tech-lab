About EduPortal

Integrated Academic Management & Digital Learning System

EduPortal is a centralized digital learning platform built for higher education institutions. Designed to bridge communication between students, faculty members, and academic departments, the portal offers unified access to lecture materials, laboratory experiment submissions, grade reports, and university announcements in one seamless interface.
